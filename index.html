About BUKC

The BUKC was started in 2001 by two Imperial College students, Will Tew and Tom Batho, who wanted to race proper 2-stroke karts instead of normal rental karts. Today it is one of the biggest arrive-and-drive karting championships in the world, with over 130 university teams racing Rotax Max karts at top UK circuits.
